# 2026-10-05

## Mjolnir pendant carving stencil, redrawn from measurements

The v3 outline was eyeballed from the reference photo and came out as a kite on a stick with a ring on top, which didn't read as a hammer. v4 was measured off the photo instead: points picked along the pendant's own axis, left and right halves averaged to cancel perspective, scaled to a 30 mm head. The result has near-flat shoulders, short outward-leaning side walls, a long V to the tip, a slim handle flaring into the head, a notched neck where the cord coils sit, and a small knob instead of the ring. The handle stays at a 7 mm minimum for wood strength even though the photo scales thinner. The old v3 files were removed.

## Large four-tile Woodsman Ways stencil

The existing small logo stencil was flattened to a 2D outline in OpenSCAD, scaled in XY only (about 2.12x) and re-extruded at 2.0 mm, the same thickness as the original, so it stays a real stencil rather than a 4 mm slab. The first cut was 1.0 mm and was bumped to 2 mm on request. Four 127 mm (5 in) tiles butt-weld into one 254 mm square. The horizontal seam sits just below the bottoms of the W rather than mid-art, because a mid-art seam left a loose island of plate under the W's centre peak in each top tile. All four tiles were verified as single connected bodies at exactly 127 x 127 x 2.0, and the source file is kept beside the models. Not printed yet.
