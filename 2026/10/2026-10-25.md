# 2026-10-25

## A philosophy quote service, built and launched in a day

A searchable, properly cited philosophy quote service went from scoping to public launch the same day, starting with 151 passages from 52 philosophers across ancient, medieval, modern, continental, analytic, and Eastern traditions. The core design choice is that "verified" is never typed by hand. A build script matches every quote's wording against a full text of the cited translation, and of the original language where one is given, and rejects anything it can't find. Several famous lines were left out on purpose where sources disagreed on the wording, on the principle that a misquote is worse than an omission.
