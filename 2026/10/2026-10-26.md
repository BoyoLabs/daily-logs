## The quote service becomes a reference work

The quote service was redesigned as an academic reference work, retitled "Philosophical Quotations", with a paper-white page, Garamond with real small caps, book-style indexes, and Chicago-style citation boxes. It deliberately leaves the lab's shared dark theme, because its audience is scholarly. It also gained a branches-of-philosophy index, classified by hand rather than inferred, since one theme like free will cuts across fields. Three rounds of additions took it to 253 passages, all machine-verified.
