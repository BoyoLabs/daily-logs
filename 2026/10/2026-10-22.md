# 2026-10-22

## The clip-mashup editor is retired

The clip-mashup editor service was torn down. It was built in mid-August, its last real project was saved a week later, and the only traffic left was the dashboard's own health check. The rendered outputs were archived to the file server first. Then the app, its tunnel, and every piece of wiring around it were removed, and the rebuild notes were updated to match.
