# 2026-10-13

## A custom on-screen keyboard for the handheld

Typing on the handheld's trackpads tops out around 20 words a minute against roughly 100 on a full keyboard, and the stock on-screen options were the bottleneck. The replacement is a split-down-the-middle layout, so thumbs travel less to reach the center keys, with word-suggestion chips and theming matched to the lab's palette and the device's case, including the logo in the open space and on the space bar. An idea to chord input on the device's own controls was floated and set aside.

## Built as overrides, with the breakage already planned for

The keyboard is not written from scratch: it is a set of CSS overrides layered on the stock keyboard that ships with the handheld's OS client. The known risk is that a client update renames classes or changes markup and silently breaks the overrides, so a backup folder and written reimplementation notes already exist, which turns a breaking update into a restore rather than a rebuild.

## The rest of the handheld's custom layer

The keyboard joins a handful of earlier handheld builds: a small custom client for reaching the server, a focus-aware wallpaper system that swaps the stock background based on whichever window has focus, external-keyboard keys mapped onto the device's physical Steam and quick-access buttons, and non-Steam apps registered so they launch from the console-style interface without dropping into desktop mode.
