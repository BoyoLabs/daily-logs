# 2026-11-10

## A chat-console outage that wasn't one thing

The chat console reported not loading while the owner was away. A full server-side check of the app, the tunnel, the edge, the static assets, the lockouts, and the login expiry came back healthy, and the page cleared on its own with no change made. The root cause was never pinned down. Page loads reached the app but got no answer back, while every server-side probe answered fast, so a transient edge or network stall is the likeliest explanation.

## Cloudflare 522s: a half-dead connection, then something deeper

Later the console came back with Cloudflare 522s on some devices but not others on the same network. One of the tunnel's four edge connections had gone half-dead, so only clients routed through it timed out, which also explains the morning's spinner. Restarting the tunnel for fresh connections fixed it, but the 522s returned within minutes, so a single stale connection wasn't the whole story. The failing requests never reached the box at all, and the box's own UDP handling was clean. That points at the tunnel's QUIC (UDP) transport being silently dropped somewhere on the local wifi/router path. The tunnel was switched to HTTP/2 over TCP, which fails loudly and reconnects instead of black-holing traffic. Whether the 522s are actually gone on HTTP/2 hasn't been confirmed yet.
