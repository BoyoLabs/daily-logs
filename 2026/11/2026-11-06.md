## A heartbeat line for the chat console

The chat console's "working" spinner was pure page animation and proved nothing about whether the agent behind it was still running, and the stream's keepalive was an invisible comment line the page never read. The server now sends a real status beat every few seconds with the agent process's actual state. A line under the spinner shows whether the link is up, whether the agent is alive, and how long since its last output. It turns amber or red and freezes the spinner if the link drops or the agent dies. Verified by killing a throwaway test chat's agent mid-turn.

## A printed slim-can coozie, right on the first try

A Boyo Labs coozie for 12 oz slim cans was designed in OpenSCAD to print in PLA. PLA can't stretch like neoprene, so instead of a smooth bore it grips the can with ten round-tipped vertical crush ribs. Only the rib tips set the fit, and the air gap behind them doubles as insulation. It also has a lead-in chamfer, a push-out and drain hole in the floor, and a simplified chip logo with a wrapped "BOYO LABS" wordmark debossed on the front. A small fit-test ring was included, but the full sleeve was printed straight away and fit correctly the first time, which validated the clearances worked out from the can's measured dimensions.

## A shareable write-up of the 3D-modeling pipeline

A design doc describing how the lab does 3D modeling was written for a coworker to use with their own AI assistant. It covers code-CAD in OpenSCAD plus Python generators, one build script per design, checks against the real rendered output, and a mandatory look at a preview. Testing its bundled STL checker turned up a false alarm: rounding vertices to 0.001 mm flagged 36 open edges on a small keycap fit-test that was actually watertight. It was switched to exact vertex matching.

## Technology watch as a standing duty

Tracking hardware and software developments, and judging their impact on the lab's infrastructure, was defined as a standing lab duty. Day to day it comes down to two plain checks: tech news and the markets. Both are awareness work that sets expectations rather than triggering actions. A self-awarded certificate for the duty was considered and ruled out, because the evidence already exists in the trail of adopt, defer, and reject decisions in these logs.
