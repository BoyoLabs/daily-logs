# 2026-11-14

## A Boyo Labs hat patch, printed in TPU and attached with heat-set pins

A Boyo Labs version of the Woodsman Ways hat patch was designed in OpenSCAD. The older patches were leather-cutting stencils for riveted patches; this one is the patch itself, printed in TPU and attached by pressing heat-set pins through the hat into its back, so the rivet holes and stencil bridges are gone and the back stays flat with no pilot holes. The first draft misread "heat setting" as a heat press and came out 2 mm thick; it was resized through several thicknesses before settling on 5.5 mm, with the pins cut in half so the badge isn't unwieldy. That left roughly 1.2 mm of cover under the deepest art recess in the worst case, thinner than the ~2 mm originally wanted. It keeps the same 108 x 38 mm footprint as the older patches so the two brands' hats match, now with true rounded corners, a low-infill print so it still flexes to the cap's curve, and a dashed faux-stitch groove around the edge. The chip logo and stacked BOYO / LABS are debossed 0.8 mm rather than raised, so nothing stands proud to snag.

**Update, v1 printed:** it didn't go as planned. The pins were difficult to heat set and didn't seat well in the TPU, which differs from the experience with the earlier Boyo Labs hat made in PLA, where the same approach worked. The cause isn't pinned down yet, but the material change is the obvious variable. That's worth remembering for later: heat-set pins that behave in PLA shouldn't be assumed to behave in TPU, and the next revision should treat the attachment method as the open question rather than the patch geometry.
