# 2026-11-11

## The chat console holds on HTTP/2

The morning after the chat console's tunnel moved from QUIC (UDP) to HTTP/2 over TCP, the console had stayed stable and was being used remotely without a single 522. That's early evidence for the diagnosis that QUIC traffic was being silently dropped somewhere on the local network path. It's only a day of soak so far, so it counts as holding, not confirmed.

## A maker's mark on every design

Every 3D design from here on gets a Boyo Labs maker's mark built in by default, without being asked. The default is the simplified chip logo. It is placed discreetly on an underside, back, or base, and it is never scaled below its native size, so its finest features stay printable on a standard nozzle. A bigger chip, the chip with stacked BOYO / LABS text, or the full traced logo are used when the part has room and the mark is part of the look. The four copies of the logo module scattered across past designs (a keycap, a lighter sleeve, and two signs) were consolidated behind one canonical master to copy from.

## Stamped, not raised

A follow-up in the same conversation settled how the mark is made. It is almost always recessed: the chip is sunk into the surface and the letters are left standing at surface height, like a stamp. That keeps it from snagging, wearing, or affecting how a part fits or sits, and lets it go on a bed-facing face. A raised mark is a stated exception, mainly for walls too thin to cut into.
