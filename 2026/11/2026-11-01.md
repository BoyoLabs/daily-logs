## A riveted carrier for a multitool bit kit

A photo of a stitched two-pocket bit-kit carrier was turned into riveted, belt-worn leather stencils. Swapping stitches for rivets changed the sizing more than the seams: rivet caps and flat-bag allowances make it about 111 mm wide against roughly 72 in the original. It uses two narrow belt loops instead of one wide strap, because the pocket columns pull inward as they fill and a strap across them would buckle.
