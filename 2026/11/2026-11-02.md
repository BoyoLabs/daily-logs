# 2026-11-02

## Where the lab's output has landed

Looking across the last few weeks, the lab's main output has converged. The 3D printer and most of the AI-assisted design work now go into stencils, templates, and layouts for handmade wood and leather pieces, not into printed end products. The printer and the AI sit upstream of the craft. Along the same lines, the straight-lines-only rule for leather patterns was retired, and rounded corners are the default now.
