# 2026-09-26

## Slow internet and stuck print uploads, narrowed to the uploading device

Power-cycling the router and ONT cleared bad gateway latency seen from the server. Next was the print server, where uploads sat at "Starting..." indefinitely. A 3.3MB test upload through the public tunnel finished in about 2.3 seconds, and WAN throughput tested fine (~26 Mbit down / ~46 up). That ruled out the server, the app and the tunnel. The "context canceled" errors in the tunnel log turned out to be the browser giving up on requests, not the cause. What's left is the uploading device's own connection, still to be confirmed on the device side.
